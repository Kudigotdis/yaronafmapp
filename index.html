Recent sports tournament increase hotel occupancy
April 30, 2026 • 16:30
▼ Tap to expand

The Hospitality and Tourism Association of Botswana says international athletics competitions are providing a muchneeded boost to the country's accommodation sector. HATAB Chief Executive Officer Lily Rakorong says hotel occupancy rates have improved significantly, driven by the hosting of the FNB Golden Grand Prix and increased bookings ahead of the Debswana World Athletics Relays. Rakorong says the hospitality industry has been under pressure in recent years, particularly following the introduction of government employee pedium, which she notes have led to a notable decline in hotel occupancies.

Education
Tertiary students welcome stipend increase
April 30, 2026 • 14:00
▼ Tap to expand

Tertiary students were all smiles yesterday after government increased their monthly stipend from 1,900 pula to 2,200 pula. The funds were credited directly into students' bank accounts yesterday, bringing immediate relief to many beneficiaries. Earlier this month, students held protests calling on the governing Umbrella for Democratic Change (UDC) to deliver on its election promise to raise the monthly stipend to 2,500 pula. Some students who spoke to Yarona FM News welcomed the increase, describing it as a positive step, although many maintained that it still falls short of meeting their costofliving needs.

Local News
North west district is experiencing an upsurge of rabies
April 30, 2026 • 12:45
▼ Tap to expand

The North West District is experiencing a rabies outbreak since the beginning of the year. Council Chairman Itumeleng Kelebetseng says 191 stray dogs have been euthanised, and of the 25 samples tested, 22 returned positive. Nearly 9,000 dogs have been vaccinated, despite ongoing transport challenges. Kelebetseng says the Ministries of Health and Agriculture have been alerted, and the council is awaiting urgent intervention.

▼ Tap to expand

The Botswana Gambling Authority is set to open applications for casino licence operations in Maun and Kasane. Acting Chief Executive Officer Moruntshi Kemorwale says the requirements will be tailored towards attracting international gamblers, rather than local players. Kemorwale adds that previous calls for applications in these areas were unsuccessful.

April 29, 2026 • 17:00
▼ Tap to expand

Residents of riverside villages in Kgatleng District are being urged to evacuate to safer areas after the Madikwe River, which borders Botswana and South Africa, burst its banks this morning. The affected villages include Sikwane, Mmathubudukwane, and Malolwane. Mmathubudukwane Councillor Anthony Rasetshwane has warned that rising water levels pose a serious threat to lives and property. He says law enforcement and disaster management teams are on standby to assist if conditions worsen.

The opposition Botswana Democratic Party has urged President Duma Boko to make the forensic audit report public to ensure transparency and credibility.
