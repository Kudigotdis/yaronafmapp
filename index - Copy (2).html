About The YAMAs

The Yarona FM Music Awards (YAMAs) are an annual music awards ceremony held in Gaborone, Botswana, organised by Yarona FM. Inaugurated in November 2014, the YAMAs recognise excellence across multiple categories in the Botswana music industry.

Award Categories

• Artist of the Year
• Song of the Year
• Best Male Single
• Best Female Single
• Best Newcomer
• Best Hip Hop/Motswako
• Best Collabo
• Best RnB Single
• Best Afro-Pop
• Best Producer
• People's Choice Artist
• Social Media Award
• YAMAs Hall of Fame
• Yarona FM Icon Award
