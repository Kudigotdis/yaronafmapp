Botswana Trivia Games

This trivia collection is designed to showcase the beauty, heritage, and "Our Pride, Your Destination" spirit of Botswana. Sponsored by **Botswana Tourism**, this set explores the heart of the Kalahari and beyond.

---

## **Category 1: Geography & Natural Wonders**
*Explore the diverse landscapes, from the world's largest inland delta to the salt pans visible from space.*

1.  **Which UNESCO World Heritage site is known as the "Jewel of the Kalahari"?** – The Okavango Delta.
2.  **Which massive salt pan complex in Botswana is one of the largest in the world?** – Makgadikgadi Pans.
3.  **What is the capital city of Botswana?** – Gaborone.
4.  **Which river forms the northern border of Botswana and is famous for its large elephant herds?** – The Chobe River.
5.  **What is the highest point in Botswana?** – Monalanong Hill (Otse Mountain is also often cited).
6.  **Which desert covers approximately 70% of Botswana’s land area?** – The Kalahari Desert.
7.  **In which district would you find the world-famous Moremi Game Reserve?** – Ngamiland.
8.  **The "Three Dikgosi Monument" is located in which Gaborone business district?** – Central Business District (CBD).
9.  **Which hills are known as the "Louvre of the Desert" due to their thousands of ancient rock paintings?** – Tsodilo Hills.
10. **What is the name of the first transfrontier park in Africa, shared by Botswana and South Africa?** – Kgalagadi Transfrontier Park.
11. **Which river is the main source of water for the Okavango Delta?** – The Okavango River (Cubango River in Angola).
12. **Which town is known as the "Gateway to the Okavango"?** – Maun.
13. **The "Tuli Block" is located in which corner of Botswana?** – The Eastern corner (bordering South Africa and Zimbabwe).
14. **What phenomenon causes the Okavango Delta to flood during the dry winter season?** – Annual rains from the Angolan Highlands.
15. **Which village is famous for being the "Home of the Bakwena" and having a rich history of David Livingstone?** – Molepolole.
16. **Which lake, once one of Africa's largest, is now a seasonal wetland in the Ngamiland district?** – Lake Ngami.
17. **What is the name of the unique, dry river valley system in the Central Kalahari?** – Deception Valley.
18. **Which town is the administrative center of the Chobe District?** – Kasane.
19. **The Limpopo River forms the border between Botswana and which country?** – South Africa.
20. **Which bridge, opened in 2021, connects Botswana and Zambia across the Zambezi River?** – Kazungula Bridge.

## **Category 2: Wildlife & Conservation**
*Botswana is a global leader in conservation, hosting the largest elephant population on the planet.*

1.  **Which animal is the national symbol of Botswana and appears on the Coat of Arms?** – The Zebra.
2.  **Botswana is home to the world’s largest population of which land mammal?** – The African Elephant.
3.  **Which bird is the national bird of Botswana?** – The Kori Bustard.
4.  **The "Big Five" consists of the Lion, Leopard, Elephant, Buffalo, and which other animal?** – The Rhinoceros.
5.  **Which reserve was the first to be established by local residents in Africa rather than colonial powers?** – Moremi Game Reserve.
6.  **What is the name of the rare, swamp-dwelling antelope found in the Okavango Delta?** – Sitatunga.
7.  **Which predator is known for its incredible endurance and is often seen in the Linyanti region?** – The African Wild Dog (Painted Dog).
8.  **What does "Khama Rhino Sanctuary" specialize in protecting?** – White and Black Rhinoceros.
9.  **Which massive bird of prey is frequently seen in Chobe and is the national bird of neighboring Zimbabwe but highly revered in Botswana?** – African Fish Eagle.
10. **In the Makgadikgadi, which small mammal is famous for "standing guard" on its hind legs?** – The Meerkat.
11. **What is the primary reason the Okavango Delta is so clear?** – The papyrus reeds act as a natural filtration system.
12. **Which large antelope is known for its striking black and white face mask and long horns?** – Gemsbok (Oryx).
13. **Which animal migration in Botswana is the longest mammal migration in Africa?** – The Zebra migration.
14. **What is the name of the world’s second-largest wildlife reserve, located in Botswana?** – Central Kalahari Game Reserve (CKGR).
15. **Which fish species is highly sought after by sports fishermen in the Okavango?** – Tigerfish.
16. **What is a "Mekoro"?** – A traditional dugout canoe used to navigate the Delta.
17. **Which tree is often called the "Upside-down Tree" and is found in the northern parts of the country?** – Baobab.
18. **The Savuti region is famous for a specific pride of lions known for hunting what?** – Elephants.
19. **Which sanctuary near Gaborone is a popular spot for viewing lions and rhinos?** – Mokolodi Nature Reserve.
20. **Botswana has set aside approximately what percentage of its land for wildlife and parks?** – Nearly 40%.

## **Category 3: History & National Symbols**
*From the "Bechuanaland Protectorate" to one of Africa's most stable democracies.*

1.  **On what date did Botswana gain independence from Britain?** – September 30, 1966.
2.  **Who was the first President of Botswana?** – Sir Seretse Khama.
3.  **What was the name of Botswana before independence?** – Bechuanaland Protectorate.
4.  **What do the blue stripes on the Botswana flag represent?** – Water (Rain/Pula).
5.  **The black and white stripes on the flag represent racial harmony and which animal?** – The Zebra.
6.  **What is the national currency of Botswana?** – Pula.
7.  **What does the word "Pula" literally mean in Setswana?** – Rain.
8.  **What is the subunit of the Pula?** – The Thebe (meaning "shield").
9.  **Who are the "Three Dikgosi" represented by the monument in Gaborone?** – Khama III, Sebele I, and Bathoen I.
10. **Why did the Three Dikgosi travel to London in 1895?** – To seek protection from the British crown against Cecil Rhodes' British South Africa Company.
11. **Who is the current President of Botswana (as of 2024)?** – Mokgweetsi Masisi.
12. **Where was the first capital of the Bechuanaland Protectorate located (outside the country)?** – Mafikeng (now Mahikeng, South Africa).
13. **What is the motto found on the Botswana Coat of Arms?** – Pula.
14. **Which famous Scottish explorer and missionary lived in Kolobeng?** – David Livingstone.
15. **In what year were the first diamonds discovered in Orapa?** – 1967.
16. **What is the name of the national anthem of Botswana?** – *Fatshe leno la rona* (Blessed Be This Noble Land).
17. **Who composed the national anthem?** – Kgalemang Tumediso Motsete.
18. **Botswana is often cited as the oldest continuous ______ in Africa?** – Democracy.
19. **The "University of Botswana" was funded in part by the "One Man, One Beast" campaign. What was donated?** – Cattle (and grain).
20. **Which woman was the first female High Court Judge and a prominent figure in Botswana's legal history?** – Athaliah Molokomme.

## **Category 4: Culture, Arts & Tradition**
*The soul of the nation lies in its "Botho" (humanity) and rich cultural heritage.*

1.  **What is the national dish of Botswana, consisting of pounded meat?** – Seswaa.
2.  **What is the traditional name for the village meeting place where justice and community issues are handled?** – Kgotla.
3.  **What is the primary language spoken in Botswana?** – Setswana.
4.  **What term is used to refer to the people of Botswana?** – Batswana (singular: Motswana).
5.  **Which region is world-renowned for its intricate and high-quality hand-woven baskets?** – Ngamiland/Okavango.
6.  **What is "Bogobe"?** – A traditional porridge made from sorghum or millet.
7.  **Which traditional dance involves rapid leg movements and rhythmic hand-clapping?** – Borankana (or Phathisi).
8.  **What is "Dikgafela"?** – A traditional harvest festival.
9.  **What do you call the traditional beer made from sorghum?** – Chibuku or Bojalwa jwa Setswana.
10. **Which ethnic group is known for being the original inhabitants of the Kalahari and for their incredible tracking skills?** – The San (Basarwa).
11. **What is "Botho"?** – A philosophical concept meaning "humanity towards others" or "I am because we are."
12. **Which insect is a seasonal delicacy and a major source of protein in Botswana?** – Mopane Worm (Phane).
13. **Traditional Botswana houses are often decorated with "Lentswetshwe." What is this?** – Decorative patterns painted on mud walls using natural pigments.
14. **What is the name of the traditional blue-and-white fabric often worn at weddings and celebrations?** – Shweshwe (though locally often called *Leteitshi*).
15. **What is a "Lobola"?** – The traditional practice of "bride price," usually involving cattle.
16. **Which musical instrument is a "thumb piano" often used in local folklore music?** – Setinkane.
17. **Which Botswana-born author wrote the "No. 1 Ladies' Detective Agency" series (though he is Scottish-born, the books are set in Gabs)?** – Alexander McCall Smith.
18. **Which city hosts the "Maitisong Festival," Botswana’s largest performing arts event?** – Gaborone.
19. **What is "Madila"?** – Traditional sour milk/thick yogurt.
20. **The "Dumela" greeting is used for what?** – Saying "Hello."

## **Category 5: Economy & Industry**
*From the "Diamonds for Development" to the growing tourism sector.*

1.  **What is Botswana’s primary export and the backbone of its economy?** – Diamonds.
2.  **Which company is a 50/50 partnership between the Botswana government and De Beers?** – Debswana.
3.  **Which mine in Botswana is the richest diamond mine in the world by value?** – Jwaneng Diamond Mine.
4.  **What is the name of the world's second-largest diamond, discovered at the Karowe Mine in 2015?** – Lesedi La Rona.
5.  **Besides diamonds, what is Botswana’s largest agricultural export to the European Union?** – Beef.
6.  **What is the name of the body responsible for marketing Botswana as a premier travel destination?** – Botswana Tourism Organisation (BTO).
7.  **Tourism is the ________ largest contributor to Botswana’s GDP?** – Second.
8.  **Which town is the center of Botswana’s copper and nickel mining?** – Selebi-Phikwe.
9.  **The "Botswana Stock Exchange" (BSE) is located in which city?** – Gaborone.
10. **Which major coal mine provides fuel for the Morupule Power Station?** – Morupule Coal Mine.
11. **What is the main source of electricity generation in Botswana?** – Coal.
12. **Which organization manages the national parks and game reserves?** – Department of Wildlife and National Parks (DWNP).
13. **What is the "High Value, Low Volume" strategy in Botswana tourism?** – A policy to limit tourist numbers to protect the environment while maximizing revenue.
14. **Which bank is the central bank of the country?** – Bank of Botswana.
15. **In which town is the "Sua Pan" soda ash and salt mine located?** – Sowa.
16. **Botswana is a member of SACU. What does SACU stand for?** – Southern African Customs Union.
17. **Which international organization has its headquarters in Gaborone?** – SADC (Southern African Development Community).
18. **What is the estimated population of Botswana?** – Approximately 2.6 million (as of 2024 projections).
19. **Which sector is the government currently trying to grow to diversify away from diamonds?** – Knowledge-based economy/Technology/Tourism.
20. **What is "Air Botswana"?** – The national flag carrier airline.
