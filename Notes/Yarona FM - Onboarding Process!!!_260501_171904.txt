2. **Music**: Primarily music-focused shows with minimal talk content.
3. **Mix**: Blend of talk and music, often featuring interviews or live performances.
4. **Interview**: In-depth conversations with guests, often featuring Q&A sessions.
5. **News**: Hard news, current events, and news analysis programs.
6. **Sports**: Sports news, analysis, and live broadcasts of sports events.
7. **Entertainment**: Pop culture, celebrity news, and entertainment-focused programs.
8. **Comedy**: Humorous shows featuring comedians, comedic sketches, or funny stories.
9. **Drama**: Theatrical performances, radio plays, or dramatic readings.
10. **Education**: Informative programs focused on learning, personal development, or academic topics.
11. **Religion/Spirituality**: Programs exploring faith, spirituality, or religious topics.
12. **Politics**: In-depth analysis and discussion of political news, issues, and events.
13. **Business**: Programs focused on business news, entrepreneurship, and finance.
14. **Health/Wellness**: Shows exploring health, wellness, fitness, and self-improvement topics.
15. **Travel**: Programs featuring travel stories, destinations, and cultural experiences.
16. **Food/Cooking**: Shows focused on food, cooking techniques, and culinary culture.
17. **Arts/Culture**: Programs exploring art, music, theater, and cultural events.
