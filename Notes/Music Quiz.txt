Music Quiz

To keep your **Regular Music Quiz** (non-YAMAs specific) exciting, prizes should range from "everyday wins" to high-value items that appeal to a music lover’s lifestyle. 

Here are prize ideas categorized by the sponsors we’ve discussed, tailored for a standard music competition:

---

## 📱 Mascom Wireless (Tech & Connectivity)
*Focus on keeping the winner "plugged in."*

1.  **The "Streaming Bundle":** A 3-month high-speed data package specifically for music streaming apps (Spotify/Apple Music).
2.  **Mascom Bluetooth Speakers:** Portable, rugged speakers for listening to music on the go.
3.  **Mascom "Urban Kit":** A branded backpack containing a high-capacity power bank, noise-canceling earbuds, and a Mascom cap.

## 💳 FNBB (Financial & Lifestyle)
*Focus on the "Bank on the Music" theme.*

1.  **FNBB Gift Cards:** Pre-loaded P500 "Shopping Cards" for winners to buy their favorite albums or equipment.
2.  **Concert Travel Fund:** A cash prize aimed at helping a fan travel to a music festival within Botswana (e.g., GIMC or any major festival).
3.  **The "Priority Pass":** A co-branded FNBB card holder and a set of premium cinema tickets for music documentaries or concert films.

## 💎 Okavango Diamond Company (ODC) (Premium & Prestige)
*Focus on high-quality, long-lasting rewards.*

1.  **The "Brilliant Sound" Headphones:** A high-end pair of over-ear studio headphones (ODC co-branded).
2.  **Custom ODC "Shine" Jackets:** Premium, limited-edition varsity or windbreaker jackets with sleek, minimalist branding.
3.  **VIP "Night Under the Stars":** An all-expenses-paid dinner voucher for two at a top Gaborone restaurant, celebrating the "diamonds" of the music industry.

## 🏛️ MYSC (Heritage & Growth)
*Focus on local pride and artist development.*

1.  **Local Music "Starter Pack":** A collection of physical CDs, vinyls, or merch from the top 5 Botswana artists of the year.
2.  **Creative Masterclass Pass:** Sponsored entry to a MYSC-backed workshop on music production, songwriting, or digital marketing.
3.  **The "Culture" Tracksuit:** High-quality, branded Botswana national colors athletic wear, emphasizing "Local is Lekker."

---

To make these prizes go further, use a **"Tiered Winning"** system:

*   **2nd Place (The Hitmaker):** Mascom Bluetooth Speaker + FNBB Gift Card + MYSC Merch.
*   **3rd Place (The Newcomer):** Mascom Data Bundle + Mascom Power Bank + MYSC CD Pack.
