Media Mogul Dumi Lopang Eyes Regional Growth and Politics
Dumi Lopang, a leading figure in Botswana’s private media, recently detailed his journey from a retail-trained youth in Selebi-Phikwe to the head of a diversified regional media group
. Beginning his career as a sales and marketing manager for Yarona FM in 1999, Lopang eventually led a consortium to acquire a majority stake in the station in 2007
. Under his leadership, the station has rebranded from a youth station to a "young adult" platform to remain relevant as younger audiences shift toward digital streaming services
.
Lopang’s growth strategy is defined by aggressive diversification and regional expansion
. His current portfolio includes YTV, YM Outdoor, and a successful investment in Zambia’s Radio Phoenix
. He noted that the Zambian expansion, launched in 2014, leveraged Botswana’s strong national brand equity to succeed in a market of over 20 million people
.
However, Lopang highlighted systemic hurdles within Botswana’s media landscape, specifically the lack of independent, programmatic listenership data, which forces advertisers to rely on "gut feel"
. He also criticized the unfair competition posed by state-owned broadcasters like RB2, which compete for advertising revenue without the same survival pressures as private entities
. He advocates for the transformation of state media into independent public broadcasters to ensure a level playing field
.
Beyond his business interests, Lopang operates as a "Kingdom Businessman," viewing his professional success as a means to support spiritual and social causes
. Looking toward the future, he expressed a firm intent to enter politics by 2029, aiming to apply a business-driven "turnaround mentality" to national governance
. He remains a vocal supporter of SADC integration, arguing that opening borders with neighbors like Namibia and Zambia would create a more prosperous, unified market of 5 million people
.
