Yarona FM App KPI's
Management for a radio station like Yarona FM needs to see how the app bridges the gap between traditional broadcasting and digital engagement.

Based on your app's architecture (Live streaming, Games, YAMAs voting, and Music submissions), here are 20 KPIs categorized by business value:

📡 Audience Reach & Growth
DAU/MAU Ratio (Stickiness): The percentage of monthly users who open the app daily. This tells management how "essential" the app is to a listener's daily routine.
Peak Concurrent Listeners (PCL): The maximum number of people listening to the live stream at once. Crucial for selling ad slots during specific shows (e.g., "The Rush").
Onboarding Completion Rate: The percentage of new users who finish the setup. If users drop off here, you’re losing potential data on your "Listener Profiles."
New vs. Returning Listeners: Helps management understand if marketing is bringing in new blood or just serving the existing loyal base.
🎧 Listener Loyalty & Engagement
Average Session Duration (ASD): How long a user stays in the app per visit. Longer sessions usually correlate with higher brand affinity.
Live Stream "Drop-off" Points: At what point in a show do listeners stop? Management can use this to evaluate show content or presenter performance.
News Read-Through Rate: The percentage of users who tap "Read More" on the news feed. This validates your editorial team's efforts.
Presenter Affinity Score: Comparing page views/interactions between different presenter.html pages to see who has the most digital "pull."
🎮 Gamification & Interactive Performance
Game Participation Rate: What % of your total users are playing the "Music Quiz" or "YAMAs Trivia"?
Trivia Completion Rate: Of those who start a game, how many finish? Low completion suggests the games are too hard or boring.
Leaderboard Competition Index: How many times a user returns to the game to improve their score. High numbers mean your game logic is "addictive."
Music Submission Volume: Tracking how many artists use submit-music.html. This is a KPI for your "A&R" and "Community" departments.
💰 Monetization & Commercial Impact
YAMAs Voting Volume: Total votes triggered via the app (SMS/In-app). This is a direct revenue/engagement KPI for the award season.
Merch Click-Through Rate (CTR): How many users browsing merch.html actually click "Buy" or view a product?
Promo Feed Impressions: How many times a sponsored post in the "Instagram-style" feed was viewed. This is the data you sell to advertisers.
Ad Engagement Rate: If you have audio or banner ads, how many users interacted with them rather than just closing them.
🛠️ Operational & Technical Health
Data-Saver (Zero Rating) Adoption: The % of users using the "Social Media Bundle" mode. This helps management understand the economic profile of your audience.
WhatsApp Sharing Velocity: How often users share news or shows to WhatsApp. This is "Earned Media" and costs $0.
Search Intent Trends: What are people searching for in the app? (e.g., searching for "Amapiano" tells management what music to play more of).
Stream Buffer/Error Rate: The technical "health" of the stream. If this is high, management will see a high Churn Rate.
Summary for Management
Focus Area	Primary KPI	Why it matters
Growth	DAU/MAU	Predicts long-term survival.
Revenue	YAMAs Voting	Direct ROI on the app's features.
